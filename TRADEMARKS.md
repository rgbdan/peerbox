# peerbox trademark policy

The peerbox **code** is licensed under the GNU GPL v3 (see [LICENSE](LICENSE)).
The GPL covers the code only. It does not grant any rights to the **peerbox
name, logo or icon** (the "marks"), which belong to rgbdan.

This policy explains what you can and can't do with the marks. The short
version: use the code freely under the GPL, but don't make your version look
like it's the official peerbox.

## You can, without asking

- Build and run peerbox from source for yourself, your family or your
  organisation, including with your own changes, and keep calling it peerbox
  as long as you don't distribute it.
- Share unmodified copies of official peerbox releases.
- Say that your project is "based on peerbox" or "a fork of peerbox", in plain
  text, as a factual statement.
- Write about peerbox, review it, teach it, or link to it.

## You need to rename it if you

- Distribute a **modified** version, whether free or paid. Give it a different
  name and icon that can't be confused with peerbox, and remove the peerbox
  logo and icon. The GPL still applies: your version has to stay GPL and
  come with its source code.
- Offer peerbox as part of a product or service in a way that suggests it's
  official, endorsed or supported by the peerbox project.

## Never

- Use the marks, or a confusingly similar name or icon, for a different
  product.
- Use the marks in a domain name, app store listing or social account that
  could be mistaken for the official project.

Not sure whether your use is OK? Ask at aciddaniel@gmail.com.
